<div class="lang-zh"><p>两天直播授课，聚焦 AI 编程工具 TRAE 在 R 语言环境中的应用，帮助学员建立 AI 辅助的因果推断分析工作流。课程提供全套讲义、可复现 R 代码、模拟数据与案例文件。</p>
<h2 class="kz-subhead">课程目标</h2>
<ul>
<li>理解从 ATE 到 ITE/CATE 的理论演进，掌握潜在结果框架、反事实推理与可识别性条件（交换性、正性、一致性）。</li>
<li>掌握机器学习在因果推断中的应用框架：交叉验证、正则化、偏差-方差权衡。</li>
<li>用 R 独立实现 Meta-Learner 算法族（S / T / X / R-Learner 与 Double ML），理解各方法适用场景与局限。</li>
<li>掌握广义随机森林（GRF / Causal Forest）：诚实树、Jackknife 方差估计、AUTOC 模型评估与变量重要性。</li>
<li>掌握因果模型评估：累积增益曲线、Qini 曲线、best_linear_projection 诊断等。</li>
</ul>
<p>配套开源工具：<a href="/software/">mlr3HTE</a>（mlr3 原生 HTE 元学习器 R 包）。</p></div>

<div class="lang-en"><p>Two days of live teaching focused on the AI programming tool TRAE within an R workflow, with full lecture notes, reproducible R code, simulated data and case files.</p>
<h2 class="kz-subhead">Learning objectives</h2>
<ul>
<li>From ATE to ITE/CATE: the potential outcomes framework, counterfactual reasoning and identifiability (exchangeability, positivity, consistency).</li>
<li>Machine learning as conditional-expectation estimation: cross-validation, regularisation, bias-variance trade-offs.</li>
<li>Implementing meta-learners (S / T / X / R-Learner, Double ML) in R, including their assumptions and limitations.</li>
<li>Generalized random forests / causal forests: honest trees, jackknife variance, AUTOC evaluation, variable importance.</li>
<li>Causal model evaluation: cumulative gain curves, Qini curves and the best linear projection.</li>
</ul>
<p>Companion open-source tool: the <a href="/software/">mlr3HTE</a> R package.</p></div>
