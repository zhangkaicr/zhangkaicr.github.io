<div class="lang-zh"><p>两天直播授课，采用「理论讲解 + R 代码实操 + 结果解读」闭环教学，聚焦 AI 编程工具 TRAE 在 R 环境中的应用。提供全套可复现代码、模拟数据与示例文档。</p>
<h2 class="kz-subhead">课程目标</h2>
<ul>
<li>生存分析核心：删失机制、Kaplan-Meier、log-rank、Cox 模型与 PH 假设、时依协变量与竞争风险。</li>
<li>传统预测模型构建与验证：变量选择、列线图、C-index / time-dependent AUC、校准曲线、DCA、Bootstrap 内外部验证。</li>
<li>纵向数据治理（tidyverse 长格式）与 LMM / GLMM 拟合、诊断、个体轨迹预测。</li>
<li>联合模型（JMbayes2）、潜类别混合模型（lcmm / Joint LCMM）、随机森林动态预测（DynForest）与 Landmark（dynamicLM）。</li>
<li>比较四类动态预测方法的适用场景，独立完成从纵向数据治理到结果呈现的完整研究。</li>
</ul></div>

<div class="lang-en"><p>Two days of live teaching with a theory + R code + interpretation loop for every method, focused on the AI programming tool TRAE in R. Includes fully reproducible code, simulated data and worked examples.</p>
<h2 class="kz-subhead">Learning objectives</h2>
<ul>
<li>Survival foundations: censoring, Kaplan-Meier, log-rank, Cox models and the PH assumption, time-varying covariates, competing risks.</li>
<li>Classical prediction models: variable selection, nomograms, C-index / time-dependent AUC, calibration, decision curve analysis, bootstrap validation.</li>
<li>Longitudinal data curation in tidyverse and LMM / GLMM fitting, diagnostics and individual trajectory prediction.</li>
<li>Joint models (JMbayes2), latent-class mixed models (lcmm / Joint LCMM), random-forest dynamic prediction (DynForest) and landmarking (dynamicLM).</li>
<li>Choosing among the four dynamic-prediction approaches and completing an end-to-end study.</li>
</ul></div>
