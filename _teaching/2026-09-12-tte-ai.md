<div class="lang-zh"><p>两天直播授课，从临床背景与方法学双角度展开。课前提供 10 学时预习视频（IDE / AI-IDE 环境配置、R 语言基础、tidyverse 数据处理与可视化），课程提供全套讲义、R 代码脚本、模拟数据集与案例文件。</p>
<h2 class="kz-subhead">课程目标</h2>
<ul>
<li>掌握潜在结果理论与图因果理论基础，以及 TTE Protocol 七大核心要素的设计方法。</li>
<li>用 R 完成真实世界数据治理、DAG 绘制、倾向评分方法（匹配、分层、回归与加权）、g-formula 与结果回归、双重稳健估计。</li>
<li>掌握时变干预的因果效应估计：克隆删失加权法与 G 方法（G 估计、G 计算、边际结构模型）。</li>
<li>掌握常用敏感性分析：E 值、阴性与阳性对照、边界法。</li>
<li>具备独立设计并完成一项基于 R 语言的 TTE 研究的能力，了解文章写作的整体流程。</li>
</ul></div>

<div class="lang-en"><p>Two days of live teaching from both clinical and methodological perspectives. Includes 10 hours of preparatory videos (IDE / AI-IDE setup, R basics, tidyverse data handling and visualisation), plus full lecture notes, R scripts, simulated datasets and case files.</p>
<h2 class="kz-subhead">Learning objectives</h2>
<ul>
<li>Potential outcomes and graphical causal models; designing the seven core elements of a TTE protocol.</li>
<li>Real-world data curation, DAGs, propensity score methods (matching, stratification, regression, weighting), the g-formula and doubly robust estimation in R.</li>
<li>Time-varying interventions: clone-censor-weighting and g-methods (g-estimation, g-computation, marginal structural models).</li>
<li>Sensitivity analyses: E-values, negative and positive controls, bounding methods.</li>
<li>Designing and completing an independent TTE study in R, including the writing workflow.</li>
</ul></div>
