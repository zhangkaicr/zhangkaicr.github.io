<div class="lang-zh">
<p>由中国医师协会、中国医师协会循证医学专业委员会主办，首都医科大学附属北京儿童医院承办，北京大学公共卫生学院协办的「中国医师协会 2026 年循证医学专业委员会年会」将于 2026 年 12 月 18–20 日在北京召开，主题为「数据技术创新临床研究，循证决策促进证据转化」。本人投稿《基于目标试验模拟与因果机器学习的肝内胆管癌免疫化疗个体化策略》经大会审稿录用，交流形式为<strong>现场演讲</strong>。</p>
<h2 class="kz-subhead">报告要点</h2>
<ul>
<li>以目标试验模拟框架写出假设试验协议，明确入组标准、治疗策略、时间零点、结局与因果对比，使观察性数据回答一个可识别的因果问题。</li>
<li>以因果机器学习估计异质性处理效应（HTE / CATE），识别可能从免疫化疗方案中获益更多的患者亚组，回应「谁更适合哪种治疗」的个体化决策问题。</li>
<li>结合循证医学年会的主题，讨论真实世界证据从方法学到临床决策转化的可行路径与局限。</li>
</ul>

<div class="lang-en">
<p>The 2026 Annual Meeting of the Evidence-Based Medicine Committee, Chinese Medical Doctor Association will be held in Beijing on 18–20 December 2026, organised by the Chinese Medical Doctor Association and its Evidence-Based Medicine Committee, hosted by Beijing Children's Hospital, Capital Medical University and co-organised by the School of Public Health, Peking University. The theme is "Data technology innovates clinical research; evidence-based decisions accelerate evidence translation." My submission, "Individualized Immunochemotherapy for Intrahepatic Cholangiocarcinoma via Target Trial Emulation and Causal Machine Learning," has been accepted for an <strong>oral presentation</strong>.</p>
<h2 class="kz-subhead">Talk at a glance</h2>
<ul>
<li>Specify a hypothetical trial protocol under the target trial emulation framework — eligibility, treatment strategies, time zero, outcomes and the causal contrast — so that observational data can answer an identifiable causal question.</li>
<li>Use causal machine learning to estimate heterogeneous treatment effects (HTE / CATE) and identify subgroups likely to benefit most from immunochemotherapy, addressing the individualized question of who should receive which treatment.</li>
<li>In line with the meeting theme, discuss feasible paths and limitations in translating real-world evidence from methodology into clinical decisions.</li>
</ul>
