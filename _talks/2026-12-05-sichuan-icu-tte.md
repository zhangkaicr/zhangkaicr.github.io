<div class="lang-zh">
<p>受四川省中西医结合学会重症医学专业委员会邀请，在「2026 年四川省中西医结合学会重症医学专业委员会学术会议暨第五届四川省中医重症质量大会」担任<strong>「案例解析：重症领域的 TTE」</strong>讲者，报告时间 2026 年 12 月 5 日 08:00–09:00。会议由四川省中西医结合学会主办，四川省中西医结合学会重症医学专业委员会、四川省中医重症医学质量控制中心、成都中医药大学附属医院承办，2026 年 12 月 3–5 日在成都召开（学术会议 12 月 4–5 日，会场：成都市武侯区渝江皇冠假日酒店）。</p>
<p>报告以 <i>Intensive Care Medicine</i>(2025) 发表的「碳酸氢钠治疗与重症代谢性酸中毒患者死亡率：一项目标试验模拟研究」为贯穿案例，按六个模块展开：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>研究背景与证据缺口</strong>：从 2016 到 2026，TTE 由概念工具走向政策支持，以及既有 RCT 与观察性研究之间的空白。</li>
<li><strong>TTE 总论 · 要素与假定</strong>：TTE 不是一种统计方法而是设计框架；按 TARGET 声明结构化描述协议七要素；潜在结果框架、可识别性条件与时变混杂为何令传统方法失效。</li>
<li><strong>研究设计 · 队列与结局</strong>：入组、治疗策略、时间零点、随访与结局的落实，以及 gfoRmula 等工具在持续治疗策略中的定位。</li>
<li><strong>统计方法 · 两种估计路径</strong>：G 计算主分析（含分步实现）与 REM 敏感性分析并行，双方法互证。</li>
<li><strong>研究结果</strong>：基线特征、未调整对比、主模型与亚组、G 计算边际曲线、次要结局 RRT，以及参数 G 估计对模型正确指定的依赖。</li>
<li><strong>讨论结论</strong>：与既往 RCT 及在研 RCT 对照、样本量测算、优势与局限，最后回到因果假定复盘。</li>
</ul>
<p>整场报告的落点不在某一篇文章的结论，而在「先写出可识别的目标试验协议，再谈估计方法」这一设计优先的思路，以及如何把同一套方法学迁移到重症医学的其他真实世界问题上。</p>

<div class="lang-en">
<p>Invited by the Critical Care Medicine Committee of the Sichuan Association of Integrative Medicine to speak in the session <strong>"Case Study: Target Trial Emulation in Critical Care"</strong> at the 2026 Academic Meeting of the Committee &amp; the 5th Sichuan TCM Critical Care Quality Conference, on 5 December 2026, 08:00–09:00. The conference is organised by the Sichuan Association of Integrative Medicine and hosted by its Critical Care Medicine Committee, the Sichuan TCM Critical Care Quality Control Centre and the Hospital of Chengdu University of TCM; it takes place in Chengdu on 3–5 December 2026 (scientific sessions 4–5 December).</p>
<p>The talk uses a 2025 <i>Intensive Care Medicine</i> paper — a target trial emulation of sodium bicarbonate for mortality in severe metabolic acidosis — as a single worked example, structured in six modules:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Background and evidence gap</strong>: TTE from 2016 to 2026, from conceptual tool to policy support, and the gap between existing RCTs and observational evidence.</li>
<li><strong>TTE fundamentals</strong>: TTE as a design framework rather than a statistical method; structured protocol reporting under the TARGET statement; the potential outcomes framework, identifiability conditions, and why time-varying confounding breaks conventional approaches.</li>
<li><strong>Study design</strong>: operationalising eligibility, treatment strategies, time zero, follow-up and outcomes; the role of tools such as gfoRmula for sustained treatment strategies.</li>
<li><strong>Estimation</strong>: a primary g-computation analysis alongside an REM sensitivity analysis, with the two paths used to check one another.</li>
<li><strong>Results</strong>: baseline characteristics, unadjusted comparisons, main model and subgroups, g-computation marginal curves, the secondary RRT outcome, and the dependence of parametric g-estimation on correct model specification.</li>
<li><strong>Discussion</strong>: comparison with completed and ongoing RCTs, sample-size considerations, strengths and limitations, and a final review of the causal assumptions.</li>
</ul>
<p>The emphasis is not on the conclusion of any single paper but on design before estimation: specify an identifiable target trial protocol first, then choose the estimator — and on how the same framework transfers to other real-world questions in critical care.</p>
