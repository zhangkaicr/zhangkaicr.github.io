<h2 class="kz-subhead">关于我</h2>

<p>临床医学本科毕业于华北理工大学医学部，外科学硕士毕业于北京大学医学部，肿瘤外科专业型博士毕业于北京协和医学院；先后在北京大学第一医院、北京大学滨海医院完成住院医师规范化培训与普外科临床工作，并在中国医学科学院肿瘤医院完成肿瘤外科专科医师规范化培训。</p>

<p>熟练运用 R / Python 进行临床数据分析，覆盖临床预测模型的构建与验证、真实世界研究、生存分析、目标试验模拟与因果推断，也做过转录组、免疫组库与单细胞方向的组学分析。研究问题多来自肝胆肿瘤与介入治疗一线。ORCID 收录 15 项成果，见于 <i>International Journal of Surgery</i>、<i>JHEP Reports</i>、<i>Frontiers in Immunology</i>、<i>Journal of Translational Medicine</i> 等期刊。</p>

<p>近年尤其关注「设计先于分析」，同时通过公众号与视频频道把因果推断、文献精读和 R 语言实践讲给临床研究者听，强调方法学结论可以被复现、被质疑、被改进。</p>

<h2 class="kz-subhead">About me</h2>

<p>I studied clinical medicine at North China University of Science and Technology, completed a professional master's degree in surgery at Peking University Health Science Center, and a professional doctorate in surgical oncology at Peking Union Medical College. Clinical training includes residency at Peking University First Hospital, general surgery at Peking University Binhai Hospital, and a surgical oncology fellowship at the Cancer Hospital, CAMS.</p>

<p>I use R and Python for clinical prediction models, real-world evidence, survival analysis, target trial emulation and causal inference, as well as transcriptomic, immune-repertoire and single-cell analyses. My questions come largely from hepato-biliary oncology and interventional practice. Fifteen works are recorded in ORCID, including papers in the <i>International Journal of Surgery</i>, <i>JHEP Reports</i>, <i>Frontiers in Immunology</i> and <i>Journal of Translational Medicine</i>.</p>

<p>A particular focus is design before analysis. I also write and teach causal inference, journal clubs and hands-on R practice for clinical researchers through WeChat official accounts and video channels, with an emphasis on conclusions that can be reproduced, challenged and improved.</p>
