<div class="kz-cards kz-cards--three">

<div class="kz-card">
<span class="kz-card__badge">01</span>
<h3>因果推断 <span class="kz-card__en">Causal Inference</span></h3>
<p>以目标试验模拟框架为核心，把随机试验的严谨设计带入真实世界观察性数据：明确入组、治疗策略、时间零点与结局，识别永恒时间偏倚、适应证混杂与时变干预，并结合 g-methods、敏感性分析等方法学工具。</p>

<h2 class="kz-subhead">Research Focus</h2>

<div class="kz-cards kz-cards--three">

<div class="kz-card">
<span class="kz-card__badge">01</span>
<h3>Causal Inference <span class="kz-card__en">因果推断</span></h3>
<p>Bringing the rigour of randomized trial design to real-world observational data through target trial emulation: specifying eligibility, treatment strategies, time zero and outcomes, addressing immortal time bias, confounding by indication and time-varying interventions, with g-methods and sensitivity analyses.</p>
</div>

<div class="kz-card">
<span class="kz-card__badge">02</span>
<h3>Medical AI <span class="kz-card__en">医学人工智能</span></h3>
<p>Machine learning meets causal inference: heterogeneous treatment effect (HTE/CATE) estimation, meta-learners and causal forests, dynamic prediction and individualized treatment recommendations, plus reproducible AI-agent-assisted analysis workflows.</p>
</div>

<div class="kz-card">
<span class="kz-card__badge">03</span>
<h3>Bioinformatics <span class="kz-card__en">生物信息学</span></h3>
<p>Transcriptomic differential expression and functional enrichment, WGCNA / GSEA, immune repertoire and single-cell analysis — linking high-dimensional omics with clinical outcomes and treatment response in hepato-biliary oncology.</p>
</div>
